Intake concept · Staging prototype
Inside GJ Systems +

The objective

Capture HVAC service inquiries when a person cannot answer, and give the business structured post-call information to review.

Reliable intake

I implemented signed webhook validation and schema normalization in a Workers/D1 staging backend. Duplicate-event handling protects repeated deliveries; a durable outbox preserves pending alerts.

Planning with boundaries

A separate Python controller uses the OpenAI Agents SDK for structured role outputs, persistent job records, bounded retries, and unit tests. It plans work; it does not autonomously execute operational actions.

Current scope

An ongoing prototype and staging effort. Retell AI, n8n, and Google Sheets support voice intake and records. The linked demo requires sign-in; it is not evidence of a finished commercial service.

Engineering notes +

From image to comparable listings

Camera or library images feed asynchronous analysis. OCR-derived queries connect image hints to comparable eBay listings through Firebase callable functions.

Managing uncertainty

Median and percentile estimates receive condition-based adjustments. Stale-response handling prevents earlier analysis from overwriting newer results. Live estimates require configured backend services; this remains a prototype.
